# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The Playwright Field Guide aims to be a practical, welcoming engineering resource. Contributors and participants should communicate with respect, assume good faith, and critique ideas without attacking people.

Examples of constructive participation include:

- explaining technical disagreement with evidence or reproducible examples;
- acknowledging legitimate tradeoffs and context;
- helping newcomers without dismissing basic questions;
- correcting outdated guidance without blame;
- keeping reviews focused on clarity, correctness, maintainability, and reader value.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, threats, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other public spaces representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Reports of conduct concerns should be made privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private GitHub contact channel rather than argued publicly in an issue thread.

Maintainers will aim to handle reports fairly, proportionately, and with appropriate confidentiality.

## Attribution

This is a concise project-specific conduct policy inspired by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant.
